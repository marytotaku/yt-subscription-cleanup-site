yt-subscription-cleanup

A personal tool that helps its owner tidy up their own YouTube subscriptions once a week. It is not a public service: the only user is the owner, signing in with their own Google account.

What it does

  • Lists the owner's YouTube subscriptions with the YouTube Data API.
  • Suggests channels the owner has not watched recently, and unsubscribes only the ones the owner confirms.
  • Organises subscriptions into groups in the owner's browser extension.

個人用ツールです

所有者が自分の YouTube の購読を週に一度整理するための、個人用のツールです。一般向けのサービスではありません。
